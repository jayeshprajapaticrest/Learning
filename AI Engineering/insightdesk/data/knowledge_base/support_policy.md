# InsightDesk Support & SLA Policy

## Support channels
Free plan: community forum only. Pro plan: email support with a 24-hour
first-response target. Enterprise: dedicated Slack channel and 1-hour response
target for high-priority incidents.

## Ticket priorities
- **High**: production is down or a security issue. Target response: 1 hour (Enterprise), 24 hours (Pro).
- **Medium**: degraded functionality with a workaround.
- **Low**: questions, feature requests, cosmetic issues.

## Escalation
A ticket is escalated to a senior engineer if it remains unresolved past twice
its SLA target. Customers can request escalation at any time.

## Data retention
Support ticket data is retained for 24 months and then anonymized.
