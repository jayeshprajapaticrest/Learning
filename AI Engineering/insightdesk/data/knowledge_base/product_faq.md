# InsightDesk Product FAQ

## What is InsightDesk?
InsightDesk is an AI-powered customer support and research assistant. It answers
questions from your internal documentation, looks up support tickets, and routes
complex requests across specialized agents.

## What integrations are supported?
InsightDesk integrates with Slack, email, and any MCP-compatible tool server.
Custom tools can be added by exposing them over the Model Context Protocol.

## How does InsightDesk keep answers grounded?
Answers about the product, billing, or policy are grounded in the internal
knowledge base using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Retrieved passages
are cited by source path so answers are auditable.

## Which languages are supported?
The assistant responds in English by default and can be configured for other
languages on request.
