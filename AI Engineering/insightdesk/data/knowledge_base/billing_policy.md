# InsightDesk Billing & Plans

## Plans
- **Free**: 1 seat, 100 assistant messages/month, community support.
- **Pro**: $20/seat/month, 5,000 messages/month, email support, MCP tools.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, unlimited messages, SSO, dedicated support.

## Overage charges
On the Pro plan, messages beyond the monthly 5,000 allowance are billed at
$0.004 per message. Enterprise plans have no overage charges.

## Refund policy
Monthly plans can be cancelled any time and are refundable within 14 days of the
most recent charge. Annual plans are non-refundable after 30 days.

## Taxes
Prices listed exclude applicable taxes. Tax is calculated at checkout based on
billing address.
